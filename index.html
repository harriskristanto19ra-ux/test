Manifesto

一環一環，皆有實證

A chain never fails all at once. It fails at the one link nobody checked. We exist to retire that link. Every ring we forge is cycled and pull-tested until the chain has no weakest point, only proven ones.

From our forge in Taichung to lifting decks in 38 markets, every Safechain component ships with one promise: it holds, or it never leaves the building.

Method

Three gates. No exceptions.

M·01

Alloy & geometry

Steel, section and weld are engineered together. We prototype in-house and reject designs that only pass on paper.

M·02

Test to failure

Static pull, fatigue cycling, salt spray, proof loads. We break our own chain daily so the field never does.

M·03

Certify & trace

Serialised links, batch records and third-party certification: every ring answers for where it came from.

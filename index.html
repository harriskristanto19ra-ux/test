Manifesto

安全，是一種工藝

Safety is invisible when it works. No one applauds the anchor that holds, the gate that closes, the stitch that doesn't slip. We think that's the point. For over two decades we have engineered hardware that disappears into the job — so the only thing anyone notices is the work getting done.

From our factory floor in Taiwan to work sites in 38 markets, every SAFELINK component carries the same promise: it holds, or it never leaves the building.

Method

Three gates. No exceptions.

M·01

Design & metallurgy

Geometry, alloy and finish are engineered together. We prototype in-house and reject designs that only pass on paper.

M·02

Test to failure

Static pull, dynamic drop, salt spray, gate cycling. We break our own hardware daily so the field never does.

M·03

Certify & trace

Serialised components, batch records and third-party certification — every unit answers for where it came from.

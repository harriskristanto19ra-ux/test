# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Today the site's visitors are people judging the craft: potential clients, collaborators, and anyone the owner shows it to. The brand itself is a placeholder for a future real business in industrial safety hardware; when that business materializes, the primary audience becomes B2B buyers (distributors, safety officers, OEM partners) and this file's placeholder facts get replaced with real ones.

## Product Purpose

A single-page brand website for SAFECHAIN (安全鏈), a Taiwan-based safety connection hardware identity. Right now its job is to impress as a piece of design: a visitor should remember the page and ask who made it. It doubles as the foundation the future real business will pour genuine content into.

## Positioning

Tested beyond the standard. Every section reinforces proof over adjectives: this brand out-tests everyone, and the evidence is the argument.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: an email enquiry (currently the placeholder hello@safechain.example). Secondary: the "Explore systems" anchor into the catalog list.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: tested beyond the standard.
- Belief ladder: (1) this company makes hardware people trust with their lives; (2) it proves that trust with testing and traceability, not adjectives; (3) it is a credible engineering partner worth contacting.
- Proof on hand: none yet. All statistics, certifications, and claims on the page are invented placeholders; before real-world use they must be replaced with verifiable test data, certificates, and references.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy. Engineering-lab energy: the voice of someone who measures twice and speaks once. Confidence is shown through restraint and evidence, never through volume.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate B2B: template heroes, stock handshake photos, blue-gradient trust badges.
- Flashy SaaS startup: gradient glows, floating glass cards, hero metrics, dark-purple everything.
- Dense industrial catalog: spec-table walls and part numbers with no story or craft.

## Design Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: show tests, numbers, and traceability instead of claiming quality.
2. Strength through restraint: the quieter the page, the stronger the hardware reads.
3. Craft is the brand: precision in typography and spacing stands in for precision in metallurgy.
4. Taiwan is a feature: bilingual accents and origin cues are identity, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the bar: text contrast at 4.5:1 or better, full keyboard navigability with visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation, and touch targets sized for real hands.
